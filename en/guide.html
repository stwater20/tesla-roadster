01Controls

Roadster Rush is a free 3D racing game that runs in any modern browser on phones and computers. The car drives forward on its own — you focus on dodging, boosting and flying.

02How scoring works

Your score is distance driven plus stunt bonuses — and every stunt bonus is multiplied by your current COMBO.

StuntHowBase points
NEAR MISSPass another car closely at speed100
SUPER CLOSEPass with less than half a metre to spare250
Bad driver dodgeNear-miss a car tagged BAD DRIVER200–500
Wrong-way dodgeSlip past an oncoming wrong-way driver500–900
AIR / FLIGHTAir time from ramps or thrusters350 per second
BARREL ROLLComplete a full roll after a ramp1,500 per roll
JUMP OVERFly directly over another vehicle400

The COMBO resets after 4 seconds without a new stunt, or immediately when you scrape the barrier. Distance points are worth 50% more while BOOST is on.

03Ten high-score tips

  1. Shave the paint: NEAR MISS triggers within about a metre. Brush past other cars — SUPER CLOSE pays two and a half times as much.
  2. Keep the chain alive: back-to-back near misses stack the multiplier. Denser traffic makes long chains easier.
  3. Save BOOST for open road: BOOST is fast but hard to steer through traffic. Fire it when the gaps open up.
  4. Near misses refuel you: every near miss tops up both BOOST and thruster fuel — the braver you are, the more you can use.
  5. Always roll off ramps: one BARREL ROLL is 1,500 points. The car auto-straightens near the ground, so don't panic.
  6. Hit the blue pads: boost pads refill 35% BOOST instantly and are great for linking combos.
  7. Fly over walls of cars: when every lane is blocked, hold JET and hop over — you'll grab a JUMP OVER too.
  8. Stay off the barrier: scraping slows you down and kills your combo.
  9. Look far ahead: watch traffic and BAD DRIVER tags 100 m out and move to an empty lane early.
  10. Lag on mobile? Use SD: switch to smooth mode at the top right for snappier controls.

04Thruster flight

The thrusters are a nod to the new Roadster's cold-gas thrusters. Hold JET and four nozzles under the car blast white jets, lifting it about 6 metres into a hover.

What you can do in the air

  • Left / right becomes thrust vectoring, so you can change lanes mid-air.
  • The car keeps itself level, and lands gently when you let go.
  • Passing over a car scores a JUMP OVER; landing scores FLIGHT points by air time.

Managing fuel

A full tank lasts about 3.5 seconds of thrust. It recharges on the ground and gains 8% per near miss. Once it runs dry it must recharge to 15% before it fires again, so don't wait until the last second.

05Bad drivers and how to beat them

After the first kilometre, cars tagged with a red BAD DRIVER label start to appear, and they get more common the farther you go. They flash amber before doing something dangerous, giving you a moment to react.

Dodging a bad driver scores double. Experts deliberately brush past them to farm points.

06Difficulty curve

The game ramps up gradually: about 11 cars at the start with a guaranteed open lane for the first kilometre, around 20 by 2 km and over 30 later on. Top speed climbs from about 223 km/h to 330 km/h, and BOOST pushes past 450 km/h.

08FAQ

Is Roadster Rush free?

Yes — no account, no app. On mobile you can use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” to play full-screen like an app.

The screen says 3D acceleration is unavailable.

Hardware acceleration is usually turned off. In Chrome, go to Settings → System, enable “Use graphics acceleration when available” and relaunch.

Are my scores uploaded?

No. Your leaderboard and achievements are stored only in your own browser.

Is this an official Tesla game?

No. Roadster Rush is an unofficial fan game and is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.
